Jesterhead

The In Flames mascot joined Legacy of the Beast in July 2023 with the In The Dark event: a hooded figure whose face is a clock, with a staff and three pocket watches on chains, surrounded by drifting ghost faces. His three abilities are named after In Flames songs.

Only for the Weak

  • Deal one hit of magic and true damage to all enemies.
  • 50% Chance to Critically Strike.
  • If any enemy is suffering from Bleed effects, including Silver and Innate effects, inflict Perfect Crave for 2 turns on a random enemy.
  • Grant a random Shield Effect for 3 turns to each ally.

  • Deal 2 hits of magic damage to all enemies.

  • Inflict Bleed for 2 turns on all enemies.

In The Dark

“Magma Beast Eddie teams up with Jesterhead to take care of Demon Princes' plots.” The event’s dungeon had four difficulties, each a run of five battles; the story below is the one told in the Normal run.

The five battles take place in three arenas, each with a past, a present and a future version and its own lighting. Every battle played the instrumental of Iron Maiden’s “Caught Somewhere in Time” (Caught_SomeWhere_In_Time_Backing_Track_V4 in the game data); the music is not included in this archive.

Enchanted Sigils, the event currency: Earned from the In The Dark Dungeon. Spend currency in Events tab in store.

Notes

How it was rebuilt

The game (Roadhouse Interactive, then Nodding Frog) closed on 31 December 2024 and its content servers went offline with it. Everything here comes from the download cache of a phone where the game was still installed (193 Unity asset bundles, 3.5 GB) and from the game’s application package.

  1. Model, skeleton and the 11 animation clips were read from the bundles and assembled into a skinned glTF file.
  2. The game’s shaders were exported as compiled GLSL and ported as they are, for the character, the particles and the arenas (baked vertex lighting and distance fog ramps).
  3. Which effect, sound or camera plays when comes from the game’s scripts, recovered from the IL2CPP binary and read instruction by instruction, and from the character, ability and quest data.
  4. Names, descriptions and dialogue come from the game’s own data tables and English text.

Fidelity

PartStatus
Mesh, textures, animationsExact Extracted data (curves resampled at 30 fps). The viewer loads lighter copies (WebP textures, redundant keyframes removed); the full-quality files are in the repository.
Character and arena shadingPorted Same formulas as the game shaders.
Arena layout, lighting, time periodsExact Scene data, baked colours and fog ramps.
Effect and sound timing, battle cameraPorted Logic read from the game scripts and data, including the camera moves of Take This Life (battle camera on).
Particle effectsRe-simulated Same parameters; Unity’s engine code is not public, so motion is close but not frame-identical.
Watch chainsApproximated PhysX joints replaced by a rope simulation with the same lengths.
Sound mixApproximated Clips and volumes are the game’s; the mixer settings are not reproduced.
Enemy line-upsFrom footage Sent by the game servers, not in the cached data; read from a recording of the Normal run. Idle animation only.

Details, with the addresses of the game functions involved: docs/technical-notes.md in the repository.

About this archive

An unofficial, non-commercial preservation archive. Source code, extraction scripts and technical notes: github.com/KirianCaumes/In-Flames-Iron-Maiden-Legacy-of-the-Beast-Archive.

The extraction, the reconstruction and this site were generated by Claude (Anthropic), working in Claude Code.

Tools

  • UnityPy and AssetRipper to read the bundles
  • Il2CppDumper and Capstone to read the game code
  • three.js r170 for rendering (bundled, MIT) · IBM Plex fonts (bundled, OFL)
